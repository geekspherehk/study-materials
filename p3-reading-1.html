🦊 The Brave Little Fox

P3 英文阅读理解 · R1 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

In a sunny forest, there lived a little fox named Finn. Finn was smaller than the other foxes. He could not run as fast or jump as high. The big foxes often laughed at him. This made Finn sad, but he never gave up.

One day, Finn was walking by the river. He heard a loud cry. A baby rabbit had fallen into the water! The rabbit was struggling and calling for help. The other animals stood by the river, but no one knew what to do. The river was deep and the current was strong.

Finn looked at the river. He was scared, but he knew he had to do something. He saw a long branch on the ground. He quickly pushed the branch into the water. "Grab this!" he shouted. The rabbit grabbed the branch. Finn pulled and pulled with all his strength. Slowly, the rabbit came to the edge of the river. Finn helped the rabbit climb out.

The rabbit was safe! All the animals cheered. The big foxes came over and said, "You are small, Finn, but you are very brave. We were wrong to laugh at you." Finn smiled. He learned that being brave is more important than being big or fast.

From that day on, all the animals in the forest respected Finn. He became known as the bravest fox in the forest.
