💌 A Letter from Grandma

P3 英文阅读理解 · R2 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

Amy was tidying her bedroom when she heard the mail slot clatter. She ran downstairs and spotted a white envelope on the doormat. The handwriting was elegant and curly — it was a letter from Grandma! Amy's heart leaped with joy as she tore open the envelope carefully.

Grandma lived in a small village in New Zealand. She had snow-white hair and wore round glasses. Every summer, Amy visited her during the holidays, but this year Grandma wrote that she was coming to Hong Kong instead! "I have booked my flight on the 20th of December," Grandma wrote. "We can visit the Peak together and eat dim sum at your favourite restaurant."

Amy could not stop smiling. She ran to show the letter to her mother. "Mum! Grandma is coming for Christmas!" she shouted. Her mother smiled warmly and said, "That's wonderful, dear. We should prepare a welcome gift for her." Amy thought hard. Grandma loved gardening, so Amy decided to pick some fresh flowers from their balcony garden and arrange them in a pretty vase.

The next few weeks were filled with excitement. Amy helped her mother clean the guest room and put fresh sheets on the bed. She also drew a colourful card with a picture of Victoria Harbour and wrote inside: "Welcome to Hong Kong, Grandma! I love you."

Finally, the big day arrived. Amy and her parents went to the airport. When Grandma walked through the arrival gate with her suitcase, Amy waved frantically. Grandma rushed over and gave Amy a warm, tight hug. "I have missed you so much, my dear," Grandma whispered. Amy felt a bubble of happiness inside her chest. She knew this holiday would be unforgettable.
