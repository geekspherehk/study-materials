🚌 The Big Red Bus

P2 英文阅读理解 · R5 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

Every morning, Sam rides the big red school bus. The bus driver is Mr. Bell. He has a big smile and a loud honk. He always says, "Good morning, Sam!"

One Tuesday, the bus took a different route. "Where are we going?" asked Sam. "There is a road repair on the usual street," said Mr. Bell. "We are going the scenic way!"

The bus went past the tall buildings downtown. Sam saw the big clock tower. It said nine o'clock. Then they drove past the park. Children were playing on the slide and the swings. The bus went over a bridge. Sam could see boats in the river below.

"Look!" shouted Maya, Sam's friend. "A rainbow!" Everyone looked out the window. A beautiful rainbow arched across the sky. It had seven colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet.

When the bus finally arrived at school, Sam said, "That was the best bus ride ever!" Mr. Bell laughed and honked the horn. All the children cheered.
