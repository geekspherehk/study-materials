Simple Present Tense

I eat breakfast every day.
主语 I/You/We/They → 动词用原形
She eats breakfast every day.
主语 He/She/It → 动词加 s/es
She doesn't eat meat.
否定句：don't / doesn't + 动词原形
Does she like apples? — Yes, she does.
疑问句：Do/Does + 主语 + 动词原形
📌 动词 + s 规则：多数加 s；以 s/x/sh/ch/o 结尾加 es；辅音+y → 变 y 为 ies
📌 频率副词：always, usually, often, sometimes, never
