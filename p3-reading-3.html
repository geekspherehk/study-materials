🏛️ The Museum Trip

P3 英文阅读理解 · R3 · 知识类

📖 阅读短文
📖 非虚构 Non-fiction

Last Tuesday, Class 3A took a field trip to the Hong Kong Science Museum. The students were buzzing with excitement as they climbed onto the school bus. Mrs. Chan, their teacher, handed each student a worksheet and said, "Pay close attention and write down three new facts you learn today."

The first exhibit they visited was the dinosaur hall. A huge Tyrannosaurus rex skeleton towered above them, its massive jaws wide open. Leo, one of the students, gasped. "It is enormous! How did it become extinct?" he asked. Mrs. Chan explained that scientists believe a giant asteroid hit Earth about 66 million years ago, causing a terrible climate change that wiped out the dinosaurs. "Only birds survived because they could adapt," she added.

Next, they explored the energy zone. There were interactive displays about solar power, wind energy, and electricity. Emily learned that a single lightning bolt contains enough energy to toast 100,000 slices of bread! She scribbled this fact excitedly on her worksheet. Another station showed how magnets work. Sam discovered that the Earth itself is like a giant magnet with a north and south pole.

After lunch, the class visited the space gallery. They saw models of rockets and a real piece of Moon rock collected during the Apollo missions. The rock was grey and full of tiny craters. "This rock is older than any rock on Earth," the guide told them. Mia touched the glass case gently and dreamed of becoming an astronaut one day.

Finally, it was time to go home. On the bus, everyone shared what they had learned. Mrs. Chan smiled proudly. "Science is all around us," she said. "Keep asking questions and stay curious." The field trip was a huge success, and many students decided to visit the museum again with their families.
