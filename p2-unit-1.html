👦 Unit 1 - is / am / are

P2 English · be 动词（香港小学二年级）

Grammar 语法
Verb "Be" (am / is / are)
📺 点击搜索 YouTube 教学视频 →
I am a student. I'm 8 years old.
I + am — 主语是 I 时用 am
He/She/It is at school. She's happy.
He / She / It + is — 单数主语用 is
You/We/They are my friends.
You / We / They + are — 复数主语用 are
📌 否定句：is not = isn't / are not = aren't / am not 没有缩写
📌 疑问句：把 am/is/are 提到句首：Are you happy?
📌 口诀：I 用 am，you 用 are，is 用于他她它，复数全部都用 are
