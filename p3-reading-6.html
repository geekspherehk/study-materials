🐹 Our Class Pet

P3 英文阅读理解 · R6 · 知识类

📖 阅读短文
📖 非虚构 Non-fiction

In Room 204, Class 3B has a special member — a fluffy golden hamster named Nibbles. Nibbles arrived at the beginning of the school year and quickly became the most popular creature in the classroom. Every morning, the children rushed to check on him before the first bell rang.

Taking care of Nibbles is a big responsibility. The class created a weekly schedule so that everyone had a turn to help. On Mondays, two students cleaned the cage. They had to wear gloves, remove the old bedding, scrub the plastic tray with mild soap, and put in fresh wood shavings. Nibbles would sit in his exercise ball while the cleaning happened, watching them with his bright black eyes.

Feeding time is every afternoon at 2:30. Nibbles eats a special mix of pellets, seeds, and grains. Once a week, he gets a tiny treat — a slice of apple, a baby carrot, or a piece of cucumber. The class learned that hamsters love to hoard food in their cheek pouches. Sometimes Nibbles stuffs so much in his mouth that his cheeks look like two tiny balloons! The children found this absolutely hilarious.

Nibbles also needs exercise to stay healthy. His cage has a spinning wheel, and he runs on it for hours at night when everyone has gone home. The class added tunnels, a little wooden bridge, and a cosy sleeping hut made of coconut shell. Hamsters are nocturnal, which means they sleep during the day and are active at night. That is why Nibbles is often curled up in a fluffy ball when the children arrive in the morning.

Over the months, the children have learned many valuable lessons. They learned about responsibility, teamwork, and the importance of being gentle with animals. Nibbles has brought the class closer together. "Having a class pet is not just fun," said Mia, the class monitor. "It teaches us to care for another living thing." Everyone agreed — Nibbles was the best classmate they could ever ask for.
