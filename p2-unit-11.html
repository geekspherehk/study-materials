Pronouns 代词
Personal Pronouns & Possessives
📺 点击搜索 YouTube 教学视频 →
I am happy. You are kind.
主格（作主语）：I, you, he, she, it, we, they
She gave me a book. I like him.
宾格（作宾语）：me, you, him, her, it, us, them
This is my pen. Her bag is blue.
形容词性物主代词（+名词）：my, your, his, her, its, our, their
📌 主格做主语（动作的发出者）
📌 宾格做宾语（动作的接受者）
📌 物主代词后面必须跟名词：my book, his car
