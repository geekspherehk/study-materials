🤔 Unit 17 - Question words: How

P2 English · How 疑问词（香港小学二年级）

Questions 疑问句
How Questions
📺 点击搜索 YouTube 教学视频 →
How are you? — I'm fine, thank you.
How = 怎么样（问状态）
How many books do you have? — I have five books.
How many = 多少（问可数名词数量）
How much is this toy? — It's $5.
How much = 多少钱 / 多少（不可数）
How old are you? — I'm 8 years old.
How old = 多大岁数
How often do you swim? — Every week.
How often = 多经常（问频率）
📌 How + 形容词/副词 构成不同意思的疑问词
📌 How many + 可数名词复数，How much + 不可数名词
