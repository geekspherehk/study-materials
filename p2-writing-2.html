🌳 公园玩耍 (Playing at the Park)

P2 英文写作 · W2 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你和朋友在公园里玩。请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约40-60词）。

图片提示： 🌳 公园 · 🎠 秋千 · 🛝 滑梯 · 🏃 跑步 · 🤝 和朋友一起
📚 可用词汇
🌳 park 🎠 swing 🛝 slide 🏃 run 🤸 play 🎈 happy ☀️ sunny 🌼 flowers 🪁 fly a kite ⚽ ball
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： My friends and I went to the park. / It was a sunny day.
  • 景物： There were many trees and flowers in the park.
  • 活动： I played on the swings. / We ran and played together.
  • 感受： We had a lot of fun. / I love playing at the park!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

It was a sunny day. My friend Tom and I went to the park. The park was big and beautiful. There were many green trees and pretty flowers. I played on the swing and Tom went down the slide. Then we ran around and played ball together. We were very happy and had a great time. I love playing at the park!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, played, ran, had)
  • 先写天气/地点，再写做了什么，最后写感受
  • 用连接词 and、then 使句子更流畅
