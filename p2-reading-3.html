🏖️ A Day at the Beach

P2 英文阅读理解 · R3 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

It was a sunny Saturday. Lily and her family went to the beach. Lily wore her yellow swimsuit and carried a big bucket and a small spade.

"Let's build a sandcastle!" said Lily. She and her brother Ben worked together. They filled the bucket with sand and turned it upside down. Plop! A tower! They used shells for windows. They put a tiny flag on top. It was a beautiful castle.

After that, Lily went to collect shells. She found a pink shell, a striped shell, and a tiny white one. She put them in her pocket. Mum and Dad sat on a towel and watched the waves. The waves went swoosh, swoosh.

Lily saw a little crab scuttling sideways. She laughed. "Look, Ben! A crab!" Ben came running. The crab hid under a rock.

At lunchtime, they ate sandwiches and drank cold juice. Lily was tired but happy. "This is the best day ever!" she said.
