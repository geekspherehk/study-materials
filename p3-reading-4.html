🥗 Healthy Eating

P3 英文阅读理解 · R4 · 知识类

📖 阅读短文
📖 非虚构 Non-fiction

Do you know that the food you eat every day can affect how you think, feel, and grow? Eating a balanced diet gives your body the fuel it needs to run, jump, and concentrate in class. Nutritionists divide food into several groups, and each group plays an important role in keeping you healthy.

The first group is carbohydrates, which include rice, bread, pasta, and noodles. Carbohydrates give you energy. Without them, you would feel tired and sluggish. In Hong Kong, many people eat rice with their meals. Brown rice and whole-wheat bread are healthier choices because they contain more fibre, which helps your digestion.

The second group is proteins. You can find proteins in meat, fish, eggs, beans, and tofu. Proteins help build and repair your muscles. If you enjoy playing sports, you need plenty of protein to recover after exercise. Dairy products such as milk, cheese, and yoghurt also contain protein and calcium, which make your bones strong.

Fruits and vegetables form the third important group. They are packed with vitamins and minerals that protect you from getting sick. Dark green vegetables like spinach and broccoli are rich in iron. Colourful fruits such as oranges, strawberries, and blueberries contain vitamin C, which helps heal cuts and keeps your gums healthy. Health experts recommend eating at least five portions of fruits and vegetables every day.

Finally, there are fats and sugars. Not all fats are bad — healthy fats from avocados, nuts, and fish oil are good for your brain. However, sugary snacks like candy, cake, and fizzy drinks should be eaten only occasionally. Too much sugar can damage your teeth and make you feel sleepy. Remember to drink plenty of water too. Water helps carry nutrients around your body and keeps your skin fresh. A healthy diet, combined with regular exercise, is the secret to a strong and happy life!
