🥞 Tom's Breakfast

P2 英文阅读理解 · R7 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

It was Sunday morning. Tom woke up early. He had a plan. He wanted to make breakfast for his family. Mum and Dad were still sleeping.

Tom went to the kitchen quietly. He got out a bowl, a spoon, and a frying pan. He wanted to make pancakes. He found the pancake mix in the cupboard. He read the instructions: "Mix one cup of mix with one cup of milk and one egg."

Tom cracked the egg. Oops! Some shell fell into the bowl. He carefully picked out the pieces. Then he stirred the batter. It was lumpy. "That's okay," he thought. "Pancakes can be lumpy."

He poured the batter into the pan. The first pancake was too dark. It was burnt on one side! Tom tried again. The second pancake was golden and perfect. He made six pancakes in total.

Tom put the pancakes on a plate. He added maple syrup and a few strawberries. He carried the plate to Mum and Dad's room. "Surprise!" he said. Mum and Dad sat up and smiled. "You made breakfast?" Dad said. "These are the best pancakes ever!"
