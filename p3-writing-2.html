🚢 海上冒險 (Sea Adventure)

P3 英文写作 · W2 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你坐船出海玩了一天，看到了什么？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： 🚢 大船 · 🐬 海豚 · 🏝️ 小岛 · 🌊 海浪 · ☀️ 阳光 · 🕶️ 太阳镜
📚 可用词汇
🚢 boat / ship 🌊 wave 🐬 dolphin 🏝️ island ☀️ sunshine 🕶️ sunglasses 🪸 coral reef 🐟 colourful fish ⛵ sail 🤿 snorkel 🦀 crab 🐚 seashell 🌴 palm tree 🧴 sunscreen
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last Sunday, my family and I went on a boat trip in the sea. / It was a sunny day and the sky was clear.
  • 过程： We put on life jackets and sat on the deck. The wind blew in our faces as the boat moved forward.
  • 活动（复合句）： When we reached the open sea, we saw a group of dolphins jumping out of the water. / After we arrived at a small island, we collected seashells on the beach.
  • 感受： The sea was so beautiful, and I enjoyed every moment. / I will never forget this exciting sea adventure because the dolphins were amazing!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last Sunday, my family and I went on a boat trip. It was a sunny day with blue sky and gentle waves. We sat on the deck and felt the cool wind. When we reached the open sea, we saw a group of dolphins jumping and splashing in the water. They were so cute! After that, we stopped at a small island with palm trees. We collected colourful seashells and watched crabs on the sand. I loved the sea adventure because it was full of surprises!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, sat, felt, saw, collected, loved)
  • 用连接词 when、after、because 写出复合句
  • 用感官动词（saw, felt, heard）让文章更生动
  • 按时间顺序写：出发 → 海上 → 小岛 → 回家
