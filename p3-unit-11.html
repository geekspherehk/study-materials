P3 English Unit 11

📖 Adverbs of frequency (頻率副詞)

📖 知識點（Grammar Point）
Frequency

頻率副詞表示動作發生的頻率。放在行為動詞之前、be 動詞之後。

I always get up at seven.
我總是七點起床。
She is usually happy.
她通常很開心。
📊 頻率副詞表
always（總是）— 100%  →  I always brush my teeth.
usually（通常）— 80%  →  I usually walk to school.
often（經常）— 60%  →  I often read books.
sometimes（有時候）— 40%  →  I sometimes watch TV.
never（從不）— 0%  →  I never eat junk food.
📍 句子位置

規則 1：放在行為動詞前面：I always do my homework.

規則 2：放在 be 動詞後面：He is often late.

We always go to the park on Sunday.
我們星期天總去公園。
She is sometimes sad.
她有時會傷心。
They never eat meat.
他們從來不吃肉。
