🐄 農場之旅 (Farm Visit)

P3 英文写作 · W5 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你去农场玩了一天，做了什么？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： 🐄 奶牛 · 🐑 绵羊 · 🐔 小鸡 · 🥕 喂动物 · 🍎 摘水果 · 🚜 拖拉机
📚 可用词汇
🐄 cow 🐑 sheep 🐔 chicken 🐖 pig 🐇 rabbit 🥕 feed animals 🍎 pick fruit 🚜 tractor 🌾 field / farm 🧑‍🌾 farmer 🥚 collect eggs 🧀 milk the cow 🌽 corn / crops 🐎 horse
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last weekend, my class went on a trip to a farm. / The farm was very big and there were many animals.
  • 过程： First, we fed the sheep and rabbits with carrots. The animals were very friendly.
  • 活动（复合句）： While the farmer was showing us the cows, we learned how to milk them. / After we fed the animals, we walked to the orchard and picked fresh apples.
  • 感受： I loved the farm trip because I learned many new things about animals. / The fresh fruit was delicious and the farm was full of fun activities!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last weekend, my class went on a trip to a farm. The farm was very big and there were many animals. First, we fed the sheep and rabbits with fresh carrots — they were so cute! After that, the farmer showed us how to collect eggs from the chicken coop and milk the cows. While we were working, the pigs came over and made us laugh. Later, we walked to the orchard and picked some red apples. I ate one and it was very sweet. I loved the farm trip because I learned many new things and had so much fun!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, fed, showed, collected, picked, learned, loved)
  • 用连接词 after、while、because 写出复合句
  • 按顺序写：见到动物 → 喂食 → 体验活动 → 摘水果 → 感受
  • 用感官动词（saw, heard, ate, smelled）让文章更生动
