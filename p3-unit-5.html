📖 Simple past tense — be (過去式 be 動詞：was / were)

📖 知識點（Grammar Point）
was / were

過去式 be 動詞表示過去的狀態。is/am 變為 was，are 變為 were。

I was happy yesterday.
我昨天很開心。
He was at home last night.
他昨晚在家。
They were late for school.
他們上學遲到了。
👤 搭配表

was 用於：I / he / she / it（單數主語）

were 用於：we / you / they（複數主語）

❌ 否定句

否定式：主語 + was/were + not。was not = wasn't，were not = weren't

She wasn't at school yesterday.
她昨天不在學校。
We weren't tired after the game.
我們比賽後不累。
❓ 疑問句

疑問式：Was/Were + 主語 + ...?

Was he at the party?
他在派對上嗎？
Were they happy?
他們開心嗎？
