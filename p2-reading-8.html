🌳 The Park

P2 英文阅读理解 · R8 · 非故事类

📖 阅读短文
📰 非故事 Non-Fiction

There is a big park near my house. It is called Green Meadow Park. Many people go there to have fun and relax.

In spring, the flowers bloom. Pink and yellow flowers cover the ground. The trees grow new green leaves. People have picnics on the grass. Children fly kites in the soft breeze.

In summer, the sun is hot. The park is full of children. They play on the swings and the slide. Some children play soccer on the field. Others ride their bicycles on the path. The ice cream truck comes every afternoon. I love getting a strawberry ice cream.

In autumn, the leaves turn orange and red. They fall from the trees and cover the ground like a colourful carpet. I like to collect pretty leaves and press them in my book.

In winter, the park is quiet. The trees are bare. Sometimes it snows. Then the park becomes white and magical. A few children come to build snowmen and throw snowballs.

I love the park in every season. Each season is special in its own way.
