☔ A Rainy Day

P2 英文阅读理解 · R6 · 非故事类

📖 阅读短文
📰 非故事 Non-Fiction

Last Saturday, it rained all day. The rain tapped on the window. Drip, drop, drip, drop. I could not go outside to play. Mum said, "Let's have a cosy day at home."

First, we read storybooks. I read a book about a brave squirrel. It was very exciting. Mum read her book about gardening.

Then we baked cookies. I helped mix the flour and sugar. I added chocolate chips. The kitchen smelled sweet and warm. We made fifteen cookies. I ate three of them right away!

After that, we built a fort with blankets and pillows. I brought my toys inside the fort. I pretended the fort was my castle. The rain was still falling outside, but I was warm and dry inside.

In the evening, Dad came home. He was wet from the rain. I gave him a towel and a cookie. He smiled and said, "A rainy day can be a fun day, too!"
