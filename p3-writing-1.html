⛺ 露營之旅 (Camping Trip)

P3 英文写作 · W1 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你和家人去露營，发生了什么事？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： ⛺ 帐篷 · 🔥 营火 · 🍢 烧烤 · 🌟 星空 · 🏕️ 露营区 · 🌲 树林
📚 可用词汇
⛺ tent 🔥 campfire 🍢 barbecue / BBQ 🌟 starry sky 🏕️ campsite 🌲 forest / woods 🎒 backpack 🧭 compass 🪵 firewood 🥩 marshmallow 🗺️ hiking trail 🌙 moonlight 🦉 owl sound 😴 sleepy
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last weekend, my family and I went camping in the countryside. / We drove to a beautiful campsite near the forest.
  • 过程： First, we set up the tent together. Then, we collected firewood and made a campfire.
  • 活动（复合句）： While my dad was cooking BBQ, my mum and I roasted marshmallows. / After we finished eating, we lay on the grass and looked at the stars.
  • 感受： The starry sky was amazing, and I felt so peaceful. / I loved the camping trip because it was exciting and fun!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last weekend, my family and I went camping near the forest. We set up a big tent and collected firewood for the campfire. While my dad was cooking BBQ, my mum and I roasted marshmallows. After dinner, we lay on the grass and watched the starry sky. The moonlight was so bright, and I could hear owls in the distance. I felt warm and happy because I was with my family. It was truly a wonderful night!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, set, collected, roasted, felt)
  • 用连接词 while、after、because 写出复合句
  • 先写时间和地点，再写活动过程，最后写感受和原因
  • 多使用形容词让描写更生动（beautiful, bright, wonderful）
