🪁 The Lost Kite

P2 英文阅读理解 · R2 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

It was a windy Sunday. Amy and her dad went to the park to fly a kite. The kite was red with a long blue tail. It had a picture of a dragon on it.

Amy held the string. Dad held the kite up high. "Run, Amy!" Dad shouted. Amy ran fast. The kite went up, up, up into the sky. Amy was very happy.

But then a strong wind came. The string snapped! The kite flew away. Amy was sad. "Don't worry," said Dad. "Let's look for it."

They walked and looked. They looked behind the trees. They looked near the pond. They looked under the bench. Finally, they found the kite in a tall tree. Dad climbed up and got it down.

"Hooray!" cheered Amy. She hugged her dad. They flew the kite again, and this time Amy held the string very tight.
