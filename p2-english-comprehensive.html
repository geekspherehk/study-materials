📖 My Pet Dog

I have a pet dog. His name is Max. Max is very cute. He has big brown eyes and a short tail. Max likes to play in the garden. He runs after butterflies and digs holes in the ground. Every morning, I give Max some food and water. Max loves to eat chicken and bones. I take Max for a walk every evening after school. We go to the park near my home. Max is my best friend.
