🌸 The Four Seasons

P3 英文阅读理解 · R9 · 知识类

📖 阅读短文
📖 非虚构 Non-fiction

Did you know that different parts of the world experience the four seasons at different times? While children in Australia are celebrating Christmas on a sunny beach, children in Canada are building snowmen! The four seasons — spring, summer, autumn, and winter — each bring unique weather, natural changes, and special activities.

Spring is the season of renewal. In March, the weather starts to warm up after the cold winter. Flowers begin to bloom — cherry blossoms turn parks into clouds of pink, and daffodils pop up from the soil. Many animals wake up from hibernation. Birds build nests and lay eggs. In Hong Kong, spring can be humid and foggy. It is a wonderful time to go hiking because the temperature is pleasant, and the hills are covered in fresh greenery.

Summer arrives in June and brings hot, sticky weather. The sun shines brightly, and the days are long. Children look forward to the summer holidays. Many families travel to the beach or visit air-conditioned shopping malls. In Hong Kong, summer is also typhoon season. When a typhoon approaches, the sky turns dark grey, the wind howls fiercely, and heavy rain pours down. Schools sometimes close for safety. After the typhoon passes, the air feels cooler and fresher.

Autumn, or fall, begins around September. The weather becomes cooler and drier. In temperate countries, leaves change from green to brilliant shades of red, orange, and yellow before falling off the trees. People enjoy outdoor activities like cycling, hiking, and having picnics in the park. The Mid-Autumn Festival falls during this season. Families gather to eat mooncakes, light lanterns, and admire the full moon. The lanterns glow like tiny fireflies in the night.

Winter in Hong Kong is mild compared to many other places. From December to February, temperatures range between 15 and 20 degrees Celsius. People put on jackets and scarves. It is the perfect weather for hot pot dinners with family. In colder countries, winter brings snow, ice skating, and the magic of Christmas. No matter where you live, each season has its own beauty and special charm. Which season do you like best?
