🦁 A Trip to the Zoo

P2 英文阅读理解 · R10 · 非故事类

📖 阅读短文
📰 非故事 Non-Fiction

Last Saturday, my class went to the City Zoo. It was a sunny day. We took a big yellow bus to get there. Our teacher, Miss Wong, told us to stay together.

First, we saw the lions. The lion was lying on a big rock. It had a golden mane. It opened its mouth wide and yawned. Everyone laughed. "The lion is sleepy," said Miss Wong.

Next, we went to see the monkeys. The monkeys were very funny. They climbed on ropes and swung from branch to branch. One monkey was picking fleas off another monkey. Another monkey was eating a banana very quickly. It made funny faces at us.

Then we saw the giraffes. They were the tallest animals in the zoo. They had very long necks and long legs. They ate leaves from the top of the trees. We watched the zookeeper feed them. It was time for the seals to eat, too. The seals splashed in the water and clapped their flippers for fish.

At the end of the day, we saw the elephants. They were huge! They sprayed water with their trunks. I bought a small toy elephant from the gift shop to remember my trip.
