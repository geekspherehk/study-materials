Common & Proper Nouns
251|
a dog, a cat, a book, a school
普通名词（common noun）
252|
Tom, Mary, Hong Kong, Monday

253|
规则：可数名词可加 a/an，或用复数形式
254|
复数：加 -s（dogs, cats）；以 s/x/ch/sh 结尾加 -es（boxes, watches）

314| Verbs 动词 315|
第三人称单数 (He/She/It + verb-s)
316|
317| 318| 📺 看视频讲解：X姐妹讲语法-第三人称单数（5分钟）→ 319| 320|
321|
322| 326|
327|
He plays football.
主语是 He/She/It 时，动词加 -s
328|
She reads a book.
一般动词直接加 -s（read → reads, like → likes）
329|
He goes to school by bus.
以 o/s/x/ch/sh 结尾 + -es（go → goes, wash → washes）
330|
She studies English every day.
辅音+y → 变 y 为 i 再加 -es（study → studies, fly → flies）
331|
He has a pet cat.
have → has（不规则变化）
332|
The sun rises in the east.
非人称单数主语同样适用（the sun, the dog, my father）
333|
否定句用 doesn't + 动词原形：He doesn't like milk.
334|
疑问句用 Does + 主语 + 动词原形：Does she like cats?

346| Verbs 动词 347|
Action Verbs & Tenses
348|
I play football every day. (一般现在时)
主语 + 动词原形（第三人称单数加 -s）
349|
He plays football. She goes to school.
He/She/It 动词 + -s / -es
350|
I walked to school yesterday. (过去时)
规则动词 + -ed（walked, played, watched）
351|
不规则过去时：go → went, eat → ate, see → saw

Place & Time Prepositions
374|

384| Conjunctions 连词 385|
and / but / because / so
386|
I like apples and bananas. (并列)
and = 和，表示并列关系
387|
He's short but strong. (转折)
but = 但是，表示转折
388|
I stayed home because it rained. (原因)
because = 因为，引导原因从句
389|
It rained, so I stayed home. (结果)
so = 所以，引导结果从句

397| Questions 疑问词 398|
Wh- Questions
399|
What is your name? — It's Tom.
What = 什么（问事物）
400|
Where do you live? — I live in Hong Kong.
Where = 哪里（问地点）
401|
When is your birthday? — It's in June.
When = 什么时候（问时间）
402|
Who is that? — It's my mother.
Who = 谁（问人物）
403|
Why are you sad? — Because I lost my book.
Why = 为什么（问原因）
404|
How are you? — I'm fine, thank you!
How = 怎么样（问状态/方式）
