Hong Kong Primary 2 English Grammar

香港小学二年级英语语法知识点速查

Nouns 名词
Common & Proper Nouns
a dog, a cat, a book, a school
普通名词（common noun）
Tom, Mary, Hong Kong, Monday
专有名词（首字母大写）
规则：可数名词可加 a/an，或用复数形式
复数：加 -s（dogs, cats）；以 s/x/ch/sh 结尾加 -es（boxes, watches）
Articles 冠词
a / an / the
a dog, a university, a hot day
a + 辅音音标开头的词
an apple, an egg, an old man
an + 元音音标开头的词
the sun, the book on the desk
the = 特指，双方都知道的事物
口诀：a dog, an apple, the is special
Pronouns 代词
Subject & Object Pronouns
I, you, he, she, it, we, they
主格（作主语）
me, you, him, her, it, us, them
宾格（作宾语）
He likes her. (NOT: He likes she.)
动词/介词后用宾格
This/That 是指示代词：This is... / That is...
Adjectives 形容词
Descriptive & Comparing
a big dog, a red apple, a happy boy
形容词 + 名词（形容词在前）
The dog is big. The dog is bigger than me.
比较级：形容词 + -er / more...
big → bigger → biggest
单音节词直接加 -er, -est
常见双音节词：happy → happier → happiest
Verbs 动词
Verb "Be" (am / is / are)
I am a student. I'm 8 years old.
I + am
He/She/It is at school. She's happy.
He / She / It + is
You/We/They are my friends.
You / We / They + are
否定：is not = isn't / are not = aren't
Verbs 动词
Action Verbs & Tenses
I play football every day. (一般现在时)
主语 + 动词原形（第三人称单数加 -s）
He plays football. She goes to school.
He/She/It 动词 + -s / -es
I walked to school yesterday. (过去时)
规则动词 + -ed（walked, played, watched）
不规则过去时：go → went, eat → ate, see → saw
Adverbs 副词
Time, Manner & Place
I always do my homework. / I usually walk to school.
频率副词：always, usually, often, sometimes, never
She sings beautifully. (方式副词)
形容词 + -ly = 方式副词
I went yesterday. Sit here.
时间/地点副词
位置：动词 + 方式 + 地点 + 时间
Prepositions 介词
Place & Time Prepositions
The cat is on the table. / in the box. / under the chair.
地点：on, in, under, next to, between
I wake up at 7 o'clock. / in the morning. / on Monday.
时间：at + 时刻 / in + 月/早/晚 / on + 星期/日期
at 具体时间点；in 大时间段；on 具体日期/星期
Conjunctions 连词
and / but / because / so
I like apples and bananas. (并列)
and = 和，表示并列关系
He's short but strong. (转折)
but = 但是，表示转折
I stayed home because it rained. (原因)
because = 因为，引导原因从句
It rained, so I stayed home. (结果)
so = 所以，引导结果从句
Questions 疑问词
Wh- Questions
What is your name? — It's Tom.
What = 什么（问事物）
Where do you live? — I live in Hong Kong.
Where = 哪里（问地点）
When is your birthday? — It's in June.
When = 什么时候（问时间）
Who is that? — It's my mother.
Who = 谁（问人物）
Why are you sad? — Because I lost my book.
Why = 为什么（问原因）
How are you? — I'm fine, thank you!
How = 怎么样（问状态/方式）
Sentences 句型
Basic Sentence Patterns
S + V (主谓)
Birds fly. / He runs.
S + V + O (主谓宾)
I like apples. / She reads books.
S + Be + Adj (主系表)
She is kind. / They are happy.
陈述句 → 疑问句：Do/Does/Did + 主语 + 动词原形？
Yes/No 问句回答：Yes, 主语 + do/does. / No, 主语 + don't/doesn't.
