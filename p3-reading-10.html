🌊 Under the Sea

P3 英文阅读理解 · R10 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

Lily lived in a seaside town where the ocean stretched as far as the eye could see. Every morning, she walked along the beach collecting seashells and watching the waves roll in. She often wondered what lay beneath the sparkling surface. "Are there mermaids and treasure down there?" she whispered to herself.

One summer afternoon, Lily found a strange shell unlike any she had ever seen. It was spiral-shaped and glowed with a soft, blue light. When she held it to her ear, she heard a gentle voice whispering, "Put me in the water." Curiosity overwhelmed her. She waded into the shallows and placed the shell in the sea. Instantly, a swirling vortex of light appeared around her feet, and before she could scream, she was pulled beneath the waves.

To her astonishment, Lily could breathe underwater! She found herself in a breathtaking undersea kingdom. Coral reefs stretched in every direction, painted in shades of pink, purple, and electric blue. Schools of tropical fish darted past — clownfish, angelfish, and parrotfish with scales that shimmered like jewels. A gentle sea turtle glided by and winked at her. "Follow me," it seemed to say.

The turtle led Lily to a hidden grotto where a pod of dolphins was playing. The dolphins clicked and whistled, inviting her to join their game. Lily grabbed onto a dolphin's dorsal fin, and they zoomed through the water at incredible speed. They leapt over coral formations, spun in spirals, and raced through underwater caves. Lily laughed with pure joy — she had never felt so free.

As the sun began to set, the ocean glowed golden. The sea turtle returned and nudged Lily gently towards the surface. "It is time to go home," a soft voice echoed. Lily hugged the turtle and thanked the dolphins. The vortex appeared again, and she floated peacefully upwards. She emerged on the beach just as the stars began to twinkle. The glowing shell was in her hand again, but now it was quiet. Lily smiled. She knew the ocean held secrets beyond imagination, and that was enough. She would visit again — in her dreams.
