❓ Unit 16 - Question words: Wh-

P2 English · Wh- 疑问词（香港小学二年级）

Questions 疑问句
Wh- Question Words
📺 点击搜索 YouTube 教学视频 →
What is your name? — My name is Tom.
What = 什么（问事物）
Who is he? — He is my friend.
Who = 谁（问人）
Where is my book? — It's on the desk.
Where = 哪里（问地点）
When do you get up? — At 7 o'clock.
When = 什么时候（问时间）
Why are you late? — Because I missed the bus.
Why = 为什么（问原因）
Which color do you like? — Red.
Which = 哪一个（给选项的提问）
📌 Wh- 疑问词通常放在句首
📌 答句要根据问句内容回答
📝 练习题 — 选择正确答案
