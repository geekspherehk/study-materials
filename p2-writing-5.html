🦒 動物園之旅 (A Trip to the Zoo)

P2 英文写作 · W5 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你去动物园看到了哪些动物？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约40-60词）。

图片提示： 🐒 猴子 · 🦁 狮子 · 🐘 大象 · 🦒 长颈鹿 · 🐼 熊猫
📚 可用词汇
🐒 monkey 🦁 lion 🐘 elephant 🦒 giraffe 🐼 panda 🐻 bear 🦜 parrot 🐧 penguin 📸 take photos 🍼 feed
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last Sunday, my family and I went to the zoo. / I love the zoo!
  • 动物： I saw some monkeys. / The elephants were very big.
  • 动作： The monkeys were jumping. / The pandas were eating bamboo.
  • 感受： I was very excited. / I had a wonderful day at the zoo.
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last Sunday, I went to the zoo with my parents. It was a sunny day. We saw many animals. First, we saw some monkeys. They were jumping and climbing. They looked funny. Then we saw the big elephants. They were very tall. My favourite animal was the panda. It was eating bamboo and looked so cute. I took many photos. I had a wonderful day at the zoo!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, saw, were, looked, took)
  • 先写时间和人物，再写看到了什么动物，最后写感受
  • 用 first、then 按顺序描述
