👨‍👩‍👧 我的家人 (My Family)

P2 英文写作 · W3 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 介绍你的家人。请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约40-60词）。

图片提示： 👨 爸爸 · 👩 妈妈 · 👧 我 · 👶 弟弟/妹妹 · 🐶 宠物
📚 可用词汇
👨 father / dad 👩 mother / mum 👦 brother 👧 sister 👶 baby 🐶 pet dog ❤️ love 😊 kind 💪 tall / strong 🍳 cook
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： I have a happy family. / There are four people in my family.
  • 外貌： My father is tall. / My mother has long hair.
  • 爱好： My dad likes to read. / My mum likes to cook.
  • 感受： I love my family very much. / We are a happy family.
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

I have a happy family. There are four people in my family: my dad, my mum, my little sister and me. My father is tall and strong. He likes to read books. My mother is kind and pretty. She has long hair and she likes to cook. My sister is four years old. She is cute. I love my family very much!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 现在时 (is, has, likes, loves)
  • 先介绍家里有谁，再分别描述每个人
  • 用 and、also 连接不同特点
