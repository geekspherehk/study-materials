🐶 My Pet Dog

P2 英文阅读理解 · R1 · 故事类 · 阅读后做下面的选择题

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

Tom has a pet dog. His name is Max. Max is brown and white. He has big brown eyes and a long tail.

Every morning, Max wakes Tom up. He licks Tom's face and wags his tail. Tom gives Max food and water. Then they go for a walk in the park.

Max likes to play fetch. Tom throws a stick, and Max runs to get it. Max also likes to chase squirrels. One day, Max saw a squirrel and ran after it. Tom was worried. But Max came back with a big smile.

Tom loves Max very much. Max is not just a pet. He is Tom's best friend.
