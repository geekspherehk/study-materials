🎉 生日派對 (Birthday Party)

P3 英文写作 · W3 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你的生日派对上发生了什么？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： 🎉 派对装饰 · 🎈 气球 · 🎂 蛋糕 · 🎮 游戏 · 🎁 礼物 · 😄 好朋友
📚 可用词汇
🎉 party decorations 🎈 balloon arch 🎂 birthday cake 🕯️ candles 🎁 presents / gifts 🎮 party games 🎶 music 🍕 pizza 🥤 juice / soda 🎊 confetti 🤹 magician 📸 take photos 🎤 sing karaoke 😄 laugh together 💃 dance
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last Saturday was my ninth birthday and I had a wonderful party. / My parents decorated the living room with balloons and colourful ribbons.
  • 人物： All my best friends came to the party, and they brought me lovely presents.
  • 活动（复合句）： While we were playing musical chairs, everyone laughed and cheered. / After my mum brought out the cake, we sang "Happy Birthday" together.
  • 感受： I felt so happy because all my favourite people were there. / It was the best birthday ever, and I will remember it forever!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last Saturday was my ninth birthday and I had a wonderful party! My parents decorated the living room with balloons and colourful ribbons. All my best friends came, and they brought me lovely presents. While we were playing musical chairs, everyone laughed and cheered. Then my mum brought out a big chocolate cake with nine candles. We sang "Happy Birthday" and I made a wish. After that, we ate pizza, danced and took lots of funny photos. I felt so happy because all my favourite people were there!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (was, decorated, came, played, sang, ate, felt)
  • 用连接词 while、after、because 写出复合句
  • 写出派对前（布置）、中（活动）、后（感觉）的顺序
  • 用感叹句表达情绪（I felt so happy! It was the best birthday!）
