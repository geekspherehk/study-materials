📚 圖書館 (The Library)

P3 英文写作 · W4 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你去图书馆做了什么？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： 📚 书架 · 🪑 阅读角 · 📖 选书 · 🤫 安静 · 💻 电脑 · 👩‍🏫 图书管理员
📚 可用词汇
📚 bookshelf 🪑 reading corner 📖 choose books 🤫 quiet / silence 💻 computer 👩‍🏫 librarian 📝 borrow 🔄 return 📕 storybook 🔎 search 🏷️ book cover 🪪 library card 🧘 cosy sofa 🖼️ poster
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Last Saturday, I went to the public library with my mum. / The library was big and quiet, with many shelves of books.
  • 过程： First, I walked around the children's section and looked at the colourful book covers.
  • 活动（复合句）： After I found a storybook about dinosaurs, I sat down in the reading corner and read quietly. / While I was reading, I saw other children sitting on the cosy sofa and enjoying their books.
  • 感受： I felt calm and happy because reading is so interesting. / I cannot wait to go back and borrow more books!
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Last Saturday, I went to the public library with my mum. The library was big and quiet, with many tall bookshelves filled with colourful books. First, I walked around the children's section and chose a storybook about dinosaurs. After that, I sat down in the cosy reading corner and read silently. While I was reading, I saw other children enjoying their books on the soft sofa too. The librarian was very friendly and helped me find another fun book. I borrowed two books with my library card. I felt so happy because I love reading!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 过去时 (went, walked, chose, sat, read, borrowed, felt)
  • 用连接词 after、while、because 写出复合句
  • 写出去图书馆的步骤：进入 → 选书 → 阅读 → 借书
  • 用形容词描述环境（quiet, big, cosy, friendly, colourful）
