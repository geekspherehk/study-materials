💭 我的夢想 (My Dream)

P3 英文写作 · W6 · 看图作文

🎯 写作任务
📝 看图作文 Picture-based
题目： 你长大后想做什么？为什么？请根据以下提示，写一段短文（约60-80词）。

图片提示： 👩‍⚕️ 医生 · 👨‍🚒 消防员 · 👩‍🏫 老师 · 👨‍🚀 宇航员 · 🎨 画家 · 🎵 音乐家
📚 可用词汇
👩‍⚕️ doctor 👨‍🚒 firefighter 👩‍🏫 teacher 👨‍🚀 astronaut 🎨 artist / painter 🎵 musician ✈️ pilot 🦸 superhero 🧑‍🍳 chef / cook 🔬 scientist 🐾 vet (animal doctor) 🏗️ engineer 💪 hardworking ❤️ helpful / kind 🌟 dream / goal
💡 句型框架
  • 开头： Everyone has a dream. When I grow up, I want to be a _____. / My dream is to become a _____ because I want to help people.
  • 原因： I want to be a doctor because I want to help sick people get better. / Being a teacher is my dream because I love sharing knowledge with children.
  • 努力（复合句）： Although it is not easy to become an astronaut, I will study hard and never give up. / If I work hard every day, I believe I can achieve my dream one day.
  • 结尾： I hope my dream will come true because I will try my best! / I believe everyone can be great if they work hard and believe in themselves.
✨ 范文

📝 范文 (Model Answer)

Everyone has a dream. When I grow up, I want to be a vet because I love animals very much. I want to help sick dogs, cats and rabbits get better. Although it is not easy to become a vet, I will study hard in science class and read many books about animals. I also plan to volunteer at an animal shelter during holidays. My parents support me and say I can do it if I work hard. I believe my dream will come true one day because I will never give up!

🖊️ 你的写作 (Your Writing)

📌 写作小贴士

  • 用 将来时 (want to be, will, hope to) 和 现在时 (love, believe, am)
  • 用连接词 because、although、if 写出复合句
  • 写出：梦想是什么 → 为什么有这个梦想 → 如何努力实现
  • 用表达决心和希望的词汇（believe, hope, never give up, try my best）
