❓ Unit 9 - do / does

P2 English · 助动词 do/does（香港小学二年级）

Verbs 动词
Auxiliary Verbs: do / does
📺 点击搜索 YouTube 教学视频 →
Do you like ice cream?
Do + I/you/we/they — 疑问句
Does she speak English?
Does + he/she/it — 疑问句
I don't like it. (do not)
否定句用 don't / doesn't
She doesn't eat fish. (does not)
用了 don't/doesn't 后，动词用原形
📌 do/does 是助动词，帮助构成否定句和疑问句
📌 用了 do/does 后，后面的动词必须用原形，不能加 s/es
