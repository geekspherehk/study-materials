👧 My Friend Lily

P2 英文阅读理解 · R4 · 非故事类

📖 阅读短文
📰 非故事 Non-Fiction

My name is Emma. I have a new friend. Her name is Lily. Lily moved to our school last month. She sits next to me in class.

Lily has long black hair and big round glasses. She is very clever. She can read long words and she knows a lot about dinosaurs.

At break time, we share our snacks. I give her my apple slices, and she gives me her crackers. Sometimes we play on the swings. Lily is very good at pushing the swing high!

One day, Lily was sad because she lost her favourite pencil. It had a pink eraser on top. I helped her look for it. We found it under the teacher's desk. Lily smiled and gave me a big hug.

I am happy to have Lily as my friend. Friends make everything better.
