📖 C. Reading Comprehension 阅读理解
📄 Mary's Daily Routine

My name is Mary. I am eight years old. I live in Hong Kong with my family.

Every morning, I wake up at 7 o'clock. I brush my teeth and wash my face. Then I eat breakfast. I usually have bread and milk for breakfast.

After breakfast, I go to school by bus. I have lunch at school with my friends. I usually eat rice, chicken and vegetables.

After school, I go home at 4 o'clock. I do my homework first. Then I play with my pet dog, Lucky. He is very cute!

In the evening, I have dinner with my family at 7 o'clock. After dinner, I read storybooks. I go to bed at 9 o'clock.

1. What time does Mary wake up?
2. What does Mary eat for breakfast?
3. How does Mary go to school?
4. Where does Mary have lunch?
