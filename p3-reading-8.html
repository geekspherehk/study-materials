🏠 Helping at Home

P3 英文阅读理解 · R8 · 知识类

📖 阅读短文
📖 非虚构 Non-fiction

Families work best when everyone contributes. In many homes around the world, children help with chores to support their parents and learn valuable life skills. Chores are not just about keeping the house clean — they teach responsibility, independence, and the importance of teamwork.

At eight years old, Ethan was responsible for three chores every day. In the morning, he made his bed and put his dirty clothes in the laundry basket. After breakfast, he wiped the dining table with a damp cloth and swept the crumbs off the floor. His mother always reminded him, "A tidy room helps you think clearly." Ethan noticed that when his desk was organised, he could concentrate better on his homework.

On weekends, Ethan had bigger tasks. On Saturday mornings, he helped his father wash the family car. Ethan would spray the soapy water while his father scrubbed the wheels. Then Ethan rinsed the car with the hose, making sure no soap bubbles remained. He loved watching the water glisten on the shiny paint. On Sundays, he helped his grandmother water the plants on the balcony. She taught him the names of each plant — the pink orchids, the fragrant jasmine, and the tall money plant.

Ethan's parents believed in rewarding hard work. Every Sunday evening, they gave him pocket money based on how well he had done his chores that week. Ethan could earn up to fifty dollars. He saved most of it in a piggy bank for a new video game he wanted, but he also set aside a small amount to buy his mother a birthday present. "Earning money teaches you to appreciate its value," his father explained.

Sometimes Ethan grumbled about doing chores, especially when his favourite cartoon was on TV. But his mother gently said, "Imagine if nobody helped at home. The house would be messy, and we would all feel stressed." Ethan realised she was right. Helping at home was not just about money — it was about showing love and respect for the people he lived with. And honestly, seeing his mother smile when he finished a chore without being asked made it all worth it.
