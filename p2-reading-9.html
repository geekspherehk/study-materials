🎒 My School Bag

P2 英文阅读理解 · R9 · 故事类

📖 阅读短文
📚 故事 Fiction

Leo has a blue school bag. It has a picture of a rocket on the front. Leo loves his school bag because his grandma gave it to him for his birthday.

Every evening, Leo packs his bag for the next day. He puts in his books, his pencil case, and his lunch box. His mum always reminds him, "Don't forget your homework, Leo!"

One Tuesday morning, Leo could not find his maths homework. He looked on his desk. He looked under his bed. He looked in the kitchen. The paper was nowhere to be found. Leo was worried. Mrs. Chen would be upset.

Then Leo had an idea. He checked his school bag again. He took everything out: books, pencil case, apple, toy car. And there, at the very bottom of the bag, was his maths homework! It was hiding under his snack box.

"Phew!" said Leo. He ran to school. He handed the homework to Mrs. Chen. She smiled and said, "Good job, Leo!" Leo decided to always pack his bag more carefully from now on.
